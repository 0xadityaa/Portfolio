## 4. Steelman: failures are free and visible (the user just asks again)
With F = 0 and a retry until it works, cost per useful answer is c / s, so cheap wins while s_c / s_e > c_cheap / c_exp.
Anthropic (Haiku 5.5 vs Opus 5.5): cheap wins at F = 0 for any cheap success above 2.4% (expensive at 95%)
OpenAI (gpt-5.4-mini vs gpt-5.4): cheap wins at F = 0 for any cheap success above 28.5% (expensive at 95%)

## 5. Cascade: cheap answers, a verifier checks, a rejected answer goes to the expensive model
ASSUMPTIONS: cheap success 0.8, expensive success 0.95, verifier passes 95% of right answers (true positive).
The verifier is one cheap-model call (3500 in, 100 out). Verifier errors are independent of anything else (flatters the cascade).
f = chance the verifier passes a WRONG answer (the leak). Expensive answers are delivered unchecked.
