Written before the first run (2026-11-27).

Task: AG News, 4 classes (World, Sports, Business, Technology), 300 test articles (75 per class, seeded).
Models: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. Score = the label whose first token has the highest next-token logit. Nothing is generated.
Shots: 0, 1, 3, 8. Three seeded example sets for each nonzero shot count. Four templates: the model's own chat template, the same
template minus the final assistant header, a Llama-2 [INST] template, and no template at all.

What I expect:
1. Accuracy rises with shots but with sharply diminishing returns. 0.5B: about 65% at 0 shots, about 75% at 3, about 77% at 8.
   1.5B: about 80% at 0 shots, about 85% at 8. Most of the gain is already there by 3 shots.
2. The spread across the three example sets is large at 1 shot (5 points or more) and shrinks at 8 shots. One example set can look lucky.
3. Template mismatch costs a few points at 0 shots (2 to 8) and the cost mostly goes away with 8 shots, because the examples show the format.
   The Llama-style template and the missing assistant header hurt more than no template at all.
4. Token cost grows linearly: each shot adds about 55 to 60 tokens, so 8 shots is roughly 8 to 10 times the 0-shot prompt.
   Cost per accuracy point gained is worst for the last shots.
5. Biggest uncertainty: the 0.5B model may be near the floor without a chat template, so mismatch could cost 20+ points rather than a few.

Result vs expectation (added after the run): shots barely helped (66% to 69%, not 65% to 77%); the spread across sets was 6 to 7 points at 1 and 8 shots; mismatch cost 35 to 41 points at 0 shots, not 2 to 8; no template beat the model's own template; token cost matched (about 52 tokens per shot, 5.5x at 8 shots). Qwen2.5-1.5B was not run.
