Written before the first run, 2026-11-27.

Models: all-MiniLM-L6-v2, bge-small-en-v1.5, e5-small-v2 (query: prefix).

1. Random unrelated sentence pairs. I expect the "narrow cone": MiniLM near 0 (mean about 0.0 to 0.1), bge-small and e5-small much higher (mean about 0.4 to 0.5 for bge, 0.7 to 0.8 for e5), so a threshold like 0.8 means different things on different models.
2. Semantic cache on QQP validation pairs (label 1 = duplicate). I expect that no threshold is clean: at the usual 0.85 to 0.9 I expect 5% to 15% of hits to be wrong (non-duplicates), because QQP non-duplicates are topically close. I expect the threshold that gives a 5% false-hit share to cost more than half of the true duplicate hits.
3. I expect e5-small to show the highest cosine for unrelated pairs and so need the highest threshold for the same false-hit share.
4. Embedding one query at batch 1 on 4 CPU threads: about 5 to 15 ms for MiniLM, a bit more for bge and e5 (12 layers instead of 6).
