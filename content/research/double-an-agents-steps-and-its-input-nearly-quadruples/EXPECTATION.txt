Written 2026-11-14 before running model.py.

Guess: tool results in my sample mix average about 1,500 tokens, so with a 250-token
reply the context grows about 1,750 tokens a step.
- No management: cumulative input at 100 steps is roughly 9 million tokens, and going from 10 to
  100 steps multiplies it by about 70 to 90 (less than 100 because of the fixed prefix term, more than 10).
  Context crosses 200k at around step 110, so a 100-step run just misses the window.
- Clearing old tool results (keep 10): cumulative input becomes close to linear, maybe 75 to 85% less at 100 steps.
- Compaction: cuts roughly half to 60% at 100 steps, and the summarisation call costs a few percent of the saving.
- Prompt caching of the growing history: input cost falls 75 to 85% at 100 steps, but cached cost still
  grows faster than linear. At 10 steps caching might barely help or even cost more, because of the
  1.25x write price on every new token.
- Sliding clearing plus caching: the two fight, because every step edits the middle of the history and
  invalidates the cache after it. I expect cached sliding-clear to be worse than cached no-management.
